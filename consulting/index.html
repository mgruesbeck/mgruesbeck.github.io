Consulting Built Around Your Raise

operations · design · communications · one operator, pointed at your next round

I help seed to Series B founders prepare for a raise, run it, and deliver on it afterwards. Investors judge three things at once: the story, how the company shows its work, and whether the operations hold up in diligence. Most teams hire for those separately. I run them as one effort, so the deck, the figures, and the data room all say the same thing.

At Profluent I was the first full-time hire. I designed the narrative and decks with the CEO for the Series A ($35M) and Series B ($106M), and coordinated due diligence for both rounds and the $2.25B Eli Lilly partnership.

├──Communications The narrative and deck before the raise, investor updates between rounds, and the announcement, press, and launch when it closes.

├──Design Figures, product visuals, and prototypes that make hard technology legible to an investor in the first two minutes. Publication-grade work for Nature, Science Advances, NeurIPS, and ICLR.

├──Operations Finance, legal, HR, and IT/security (SOC 2) in a state that holds up in diligence, with a data room that answers questions before they are asked.

└──AI-first AI agents do the drafting, research, and production under my direction. That is how one person delivers the output of all three.

# CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consulting Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of __________ (the "Effective Date"), by and between Melvin Gruesbeck ("Consultant") and [Client Company Name], located at [Company Address] ("Client").

## 1. Services

Consultant agrees to provide advisory and hands-on services to Client across operations, communications, and design, beginning with: [Initial Focus Areas] (the "Services"). Client may redirect Consultant's hours among these areas at any time by email, and no amendment to this Agreement is needed to do so.

## 2. Plan, Fees & Payment

Plan: Trial, 10 hours within 30 days of the Effective Date
Fee: $2,000.00, one payment
Term: 30 days, no renewal
Notice Period: None; the Trial ends on its own

The hours in the Plan are reserved capacity. Hours not used within a monthly billing period, or by the end of a Trial, expire and are not refunded or carried over. Hours beyond the Plan require Client's prior approval by email and are billed at $250.00 per hour. The first payment is due upon full execution (countersignature) of this Agreement via the Stripe ACH bank transfer payment link provided to Client. For a monthly Plan, each later payment is due in advance on the monthly anniversary of the first payment, and the discounted Fee for a 6-month or 12-month Term applies from the first payment.

The parties may change the Plan or the Term by email confirmed by both parties, including continuing from a Trial onto a monthly Plan, and this Agreement then governs the new Plan from the date agreed. A Trial is available once per Client.

## 3. Term & Ending

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date. A Trial lasts thirty (30) days and ends on its own, with no renewal. A monthly Plan renews automatically each month until either party ends it by giving the other written notice (including via email) of at least the Notice Period: thirty (30) days on a month-to-month Term, sixty (60) days on a 6-month Term, and ninety (90) days on a 12-month Term. Fees remain payable, and Consultant continues to provide the Services, through the Notice Period. There is no early termination fee, no repayment of any discount, and no refund of Fees already paid. A 6-month or 12-month Term that reaches its end continues at the same Fee and Notice Period until either party gives notice. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately in the event of an uncured material breach.

## 4. AI Tools, Costs & Specialists

Consultant uses AI tools to perform the Services, reviews their output, and remains responsible for all work delivered. Consultant's own tools and subscriptions are included in the Fee. Consultant will use Client's Confidential Information only in tools whose terms do not permit training on it. Any AI system built to keep running in Client's business will be set up on accounts that Client owns and pays for directly. Client acknowledges that Deliverables may include AI-generated material, which may not be eligible for copyright protection.

Consultant will not incur usage-based AI charges on Client's accounts of more than ten percent (10%) of the Fee in any month, and will not incur any other cost on Client's behalf, without Client's prior approval by email. Approved costs are paid by Client directly or reimbursed at cost, without markup.

The hours in the Plan are Consultant's own. If the Services call for a specialist (for example an artist, designer, writer, or operator), Consultant will propose the person, the work, and a fixed budget, and no specialist will be engaged without Client's prior approval by email. By default, Client engages and pays the specialist directly and Consultant directs the work within the Plan. If Client prefers a single contract, Consultant may engage the specialist as a subcontractor, in which case Client pays the approved budget in advance at cost, without markup, the subcontractor will be bound in writing by confidentiality and intellectual property terms at least as protective of Client as this Agreement, and Consultant remains responsible for the subcontractor's work.

## 5. Intellectual Property

Upon Consultant's receipt of full payment for the applicable billing period, Consultant assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in bespoke work product created exclusively for Client under this Agreement ("Deliverables"). Consultant retains all right, title, and interest in pre-existing tools, code, templates, frameworks, and general know-how ("Background IP"), and grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use any Background IP incorporated into the Deliverables.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to hold the other party's non-public business, technical, financial, and scientific information ("Confidential Information") in strict confidence and not to disclose it to any third party or use it for any purpose outside the performance of this Agreement.

## 7. Independent Contractor & Limitation of Liability

Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee or agent of Client. NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. CONSULTANT'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CLIENT TO CONSULTANT IN THE ONE (1) MONTH IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

## 8. Assignment

Consultant may assign this Agreement upon written notice to Client to a wholly-owned limited liability company (LLC) or corporate entity controlled by Consultant. Otherwise, neither party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent.

## 9. Governing Law & Counterparts

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Electronic signatures transmitted digitally shall have the same legal force and effect as original signatures.

## 10. Dispute Resolution

The parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement by direct discussion for thirty (30) days after either party gives the other written notice of the dispute (including via email). Any dispute not resolved in that period shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the California county of Consultant's principal place of business, and each party consents to the jurisdiction and venue of those courts. Nothing in this section prevents either party from bringing a qualifying claim in small claims court, or from seeking injunctive relief at any time to protect its Confidential Information or intellectual property.
